# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Security engineering leads, researchers, hiring teams, and technically curious visitors evaluating Pruthu Raut's security research and product-security work.

## Product Purpose

An experience-led portfolio that makes security research understandable, credible, and memorable. Success means a visitor can quickly understand Pruthu's point of view, inspect substantial project evidence, follow the work history, and start a conversation.

## Positioning

Pruthu connects offensive research to practical engineering controls: break a system, prove the path, and harden what ships.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from professional networks, research communities, and job searches. They scan on desktop and mobile, jump between work, experience, and contact, and may later watch embedded project videos.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page Next.js portfolio with responsive web layouts.
- Project case studies need room for detailed information, a visual thumbnail, and a future video slot.
- Scroll motion should support discovery without hiding content or blocking reduced-motion users.
- Existing claims and work history are the source of truth; future videos and additional evidence remain open slots.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Pruthu Raut.
- Voice: direct, curious, technically literate, self-aware.
- User-provided line: “I like to break systems for fun and profit.”
- Subject: cybersecurity research, product security, exploit validation, and secure-by-default AI development.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing project content in `app/page.tsx`: Nightcrawler, VibeGuard, and Obsedian Guard.
- Existing experience content: Illumio, Bugcrowd, CoE-CNDS Labs, and GreenCurve Securities.
- Existing hall-of-fame references: NASA, Apple, WHO, BBC, Drexel, and Cambridge.
- No project videos are supplied yet; video areas must be clearly labeled as future media slots.

## Product Principles

- Prove the mechanism, not only the claim.
- Let visitors explore depth without losing the narrative.
- Treat offensive research as a path from signal to evidence to control.
- Make technical work legible to both specialists and strong generalists.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Content must remain available without animation, use visible keyboard focus, preserve readable contrast, and work at narrow mobile widths.
